This visualization inverts the Benefits Access Journey Matrix. Where the journey matrix asks "what happens when someone loses a job?", this crosswalk asks "where does SNAP show up across all life events?" Benefits are not siloed — they appear across multiple journeys, which is why the shared infrastructure layer (rules engines, application AI, navigator tools) is the highest-leverage investment surface. A single improvement to SNAP application assistance, for example, impacts 5 of 8 journeys simultaneously.
