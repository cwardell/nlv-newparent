Stage-by-stage navigation map from pregnancy confirmation through the child's first year

This blueprint maps the key decision points, available navigators and who employs them, systems-level touchpoints, drop-off risks, NavTech intervention opportunities, and the financial value at stake across seven stages of the new parent benefits journey. All dollar figures are annualized estimates for a reference household: single parent, two children (including newborn), ~$28K income, ~150% FPL — modeled on the Maria persona from NLV's Low-Income Population Memo.

All dollar figures represent annualized benefit values for the reference household unless noted as one-time. Figures derived from published federal benefit schedules (USDA SNAP/WIC tables, IRS EITC/CTC tables, state childcare subsidy rate schedules), NCHS birth data (2024), and NLV research. Conditional benefits (PFML) shown with dashed borders. Cumulative tracker assumes a PFML-available state for on-track; in non-PFML states the on-track total is ~$3,500 lower. Mobility experience impacts from Camber Collective (2024) shown as lifetime earnings context alongside annual dollar values. This service blueprint is structured to parallel the CJ Housing Stabilization and Employment After Conviction deep dives. Each stage identifies the parent's experience, the navigator ecosystem (who helps and who employs them), the systems-level infrastructure, where people fall off and why, and where NavTech could intervene. The stages are sequential but overlapping — a parent may be simultaneously navigating Medicaid enrollment (Stage 2), WIC certification (Stage 3), and PFML application (Stage 4). Benefits not claimed during the window of each stage often go permanently unclaimed.
