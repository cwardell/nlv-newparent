Unlike criminal justice journeys (hierarchical, cascading triggers), benefits journeys share a common horizontal process but enter from different life events. The infrastructure layer serves all journeys simultaneously — this is where NLV's benefits access market thesis investments operate. Journeys are not mutually exclusive; an individual may be on multiple journeys simultaneously (e.g., job loss triggering both UI and SNAP, while also needing cross-enrollment into Medicaid).
